UpSTREAM delivers accessible STEM education through tutoring, webinars, and workshops. As UpSTREAM's executive director, Kevin oversees the organization's logistics and operations. Previously, he also developed an engaging elementary school curriculum and mentored middle and high school students in designing the program's website.

The Social Futures Lab is reimagining social and collaborative systems in software. As a research assistant, Kevin designed a social computing curriculum, partnered with Seattle Public Schools to implement the curriculum, and co-authored a publication presenting his team's findings for the ACM SIGCSE Technical Symposium in 2024.


As a two-time software development engineer intern at Amazon, Kevin built platforms used across regional experimentation and custom order fulfillment workflows. His solutions consolidated data from distributed systems into centralized tools, improving operational visibility and supporting large-scale, business-critical infrastructure.

Project Sidewalk allows users to crowdsource sidewalk accessibility information. As a software engineer intern with the organization, Kevin improved the tool's functionality and user interface. His work internationalized the platform for users across four continents and 21 cities, including Amsterdam, Chicago, Mexico City, and Taipei.
