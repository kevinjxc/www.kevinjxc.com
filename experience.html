UpSTREAM delivers accessible STEM education through tutoring, webinars, and workshops. As UpSTREAM's executive director, Kevin oversees the organization's logistics and operations. Previously, he also developed an engaging elementary school curriculum and mentored middle and high school students in designing the program's website.

The Social Futures Lab is reimagining social and collaborative systems in software. As a research assistant, Kevin designed a social computing curriculum, partnered with Seattle Public Schools to implement the curriculum, and co-authored a publication presenting his team's findings for the ACM SIGCSE Technical Symposium in 2024.

30+ is a mathematics game developed with C# and Unity to address learning loss faced by students nationwide. Kevin designed the game to help elementary school students strengthen their fact fluency. The game has been shared with classrooms across Seattle, engaging young learners in building their mathematics skills.

Spelling Bee is a JavaScript-based literacy game to help students strengthen their phonetic skills. Kevin designed the game to support elementary students in improving their literacy through interactive spelling challenges. The game has been shared with classrooms nationwide, helping young learners practice their phonetic and keyboard skills.


As a two-time software development engineer intern at Amazon, Kevin built platforms used across regional experimentation and custom order fulfillment workflows. His solutions consolidated data from distributed systems into centralized tools, improving operational visibility and supporting large-scale, business-critical infrastructure.

Project Sidewalk allows users to crowdsource sidewalk accessibility information. As a software engineer intern with the organization, Kevin improved the tool's functionality and user interface. His work internationalized the platform for users across four continents and 21 cities, including Amsterdam, Chicago, Mexico City, and Taipei.

One is an app that inspires youth to take action on environmental issues like pollution, climate change, and water conservation. Built with JavaScript, the app is part of several local elementary school curriculums. The concept reflects Kevin's long-time passion for the environment, especially in light of the world’s pressing climate challenges.

Blind Cleaners Co. provides ultrasonic blind cleaning services in Seattle. Kevin designed and developed the company’s website to enhance customer engagement and streamline service inquiries. He also supported efforts to scale operations by improving online visibility and contributing to strategic planning for regional growth.
